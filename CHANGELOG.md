# Changelog

## 0.53.0: faster from the first click

**Your First Site, Ready at Launch**
- Ufolio learns which site you open first and has it loaded by the time you click, so it appears instantly. Just open Ufolio and pick your usual favourite.

**Quicker Social Sites**
- Facebook, X and Reddit get their connections ready the moment you head there, and Ufolio learns the same trick for the other sites you visit. Now available in 0.53.0.

**Bug fixes**
- Bug fixed where Back, Reload and zoom changed the page underneath an open Link preview.
- Bug fixed where deleting an Orbit woke every sleeping tab in it.
- Other bug fixes and improvements.

## 0.52.1: Compact Mode in a click

**Compact Mode from the MORE menu**
- Hide the tabs and toolbar to give the page the whole window, straight from MORE. Choose Leave Compact Mode in the same place to bring them back.

**Bug fixes**
- Bug fixed where a YouTube ad could play in full when you switched videos. It's now skipped in a second or so, and your speed and sound come back afterwards.
- Bug fixed where, in Compact mode, menus and popovers from the toolbar closed as soon as you moved the pointer into them.
- Other bug fixes and improvements.
